Our Heritage

Crafting Identity
Since 1998.

Shree Krishna Labels has been defining garment identity for over 25 years. From our precision woven label manufacturing facility in Vasai, Mumbai, we deliver premium branding solutions — custom woven labels, hang tags, heat transfer labels, PVC patches, satin labels, barcode labels and wash care labels — to fashion houses, export companies, and luxury apparel brands globally.

Every label we produce carries the DNA of your brand — meticulously crafted using the finest materials and advanced technology that meets international compliance standards. Our ISO 9001 certified facility in Maharashtra ensures consistent quality across every order, from sampling to bulk production.
